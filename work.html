Research

TESS exoplanet transit fitting

Building a consistent, automated approach to downloading, detrending and fitting TESS transit light curves, with an emphasis on planets around M-dwarf stars. The pipeline uses Python, transit models and Bayesian MCMC methods.

Light-curve methods

Comparing detrending approaches, investigating contamination and aperture choices, and testing methods using synthetic planet transits.

ExoMol MSc project

My MSc dissertation focused on a high-temperature molecular line list for NO⁺, contributing to research on molecular spectroscopy in planetary atmospheres.
