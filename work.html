Research

TESS exoplanet transit fitting

I am developing an automated approach to downloading, detrending and fitting TESS transit light curves, with an emphasis on planets around M-dwarf stars. I use Python, transit models and Bayesian MCMC methods.

ExoMol MSc project

For my MSc dissertation, I worked on a high-temperature molecular line list for NO⁺, contributing to research on molecular spectroscopy in planetary atmospheres.

Teaching & Outreach

Orbyts

I support school students in astronomy research through the award-winning Orbyts programme. I also lead the MSSL Hub for Orbyts.

Science education

I have taught science and mathematics at secondary-school level and science communication at university level.
